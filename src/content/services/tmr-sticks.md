Character moving on its own? Camera creeping to one side? That's stick drift. The stock DualSense
sticks wear out with use, often within a couple of years.

We replace them with **TMR sticks**, which sense position magnetically instead of with parts that
rub and wear down. Installed and tested before you get it back, usually **within an hour**.
